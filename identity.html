Brand identity

The logo, colours and type for OffshoreCorp Services. Use them together across the site, the PDFs and email, so every document looks like it came from the same firm.

Logo

The mark is a globe with a single meridian line, drawn in a single colour. The wordmark sets the name in serif type, with "Services" in the accent green.

Voice

  • Clear: fees and exclusions stated plainly
  • Measured: no guarantees and no hype
  • Compliant: screening and disclosure come first

Logo rules

  • Keep clear space around the mark equal to its radius
  • Minimum size 24 px high
  • Do not stretch, recolour outside the palette or add effects

Brand story

OffshoreCorp Services exists to make offshore company formation and licensing support clear. Fixed ranges, written quotes and honest exclusions come first, and the look of every page and PDF follows the same restraint.

Applied examples

  • PDF cover: logo at the top, serif title, one forest-green accent line for the fee.
  • Email signature: name, role, the logo mark, and the sales address only.
  • Website: paper background, one accent colour, hairline rules, no gradients or shadows.

What we do not do

  • No "guaranteed", "anonymous" or "no reporting" claims.
  • No urgency language or countdown offers.
  • No stock photography of people in suits.

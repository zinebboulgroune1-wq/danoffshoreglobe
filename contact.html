Contact

Email is our only contact channel. Send your enquiry to the address below and we will reply by email.

Email sales@offshoreglobe.com

What to include

  • Full name and country of residence
  • Business activity and preferred jurisdiction
  • Whether a virtual asset, payment or other licensed activity is planned
  • Expected ownership structure and source of initial funds

What happens next

  1. We review your enquiry and reply by email with any questions.
  2. We run identity and source-of-funds screening.
  3. You receive a written quote. The minimum to start depends on the jurisdiction.
  4. After you sign the engagement letter and pay, we begin the work.
The exact quote is set after identity screening. Indicative fee ranges only. Prices exclude government, regulator, bank and capital costs and are confirmed in writing after identity and source-of-funds checks. We do not guarantee incorporation timelines, bank account opening or licence approval.

Before you write

  • Read the Jurisdictions and Licensing pages to see the fee ranges and what is excluded.
  • Have your passport and proof of address ready for when screening starts.
  • Describe your business activity in one or two lines.

What we reply with

  • Questions about your activity and the jurisdiction you prefer.
  • A list of documents we need for screening.
  • A written quote after screening, with the minimum to start and the exclusions.

Questions

Do you ask for payment before a quote?

No. You pay only the minimum to start, and only after you have a written quote and have signed the engagement letter.

Can I call instead?

Email is our only contact channel, so the record of your enquiry stays in writing.

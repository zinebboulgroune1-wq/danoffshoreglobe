How to read the comparison

  • Fees are indicative ranges. Government, regulator, bank and capital costs are extra in every case.
  • Substance means the local presence a regulator may expect, such as an office, directors or staff.
  • Regulatory profile describes which authority is involved. It does not predict whether an application will be approved.

Decision checklist

  • Confirm the activity you plan to run and whether it is regulated in the jurisdiction you choose.
  • Compare the minimum to start and the licensing fee against your budget, including the costs we exclude.
  • Check the substance expectation and whether you can meet it in practice.
  • Ask for a written scope before you pay anything beyond the minimum to start.

Questions

Which jurisdiction is cheapest?

Saint Lucia has the lowest minimum to start at $3,500. The cheapest option for you depends on your activity and on the extra costs it creates.

Why are licensing fees so different?

Licensing support covers different activities and different regulators. The fee follows the complexity of the application, not a single fixed price.

Which option suits which plan

  • A holding or general commercial structure with no regulated activity usually points to a company formation option.
  • A planned virtual asset or payment business points to licensing support, and the regulator becomes central to the timeline.
  • A fund or investment-related plan usually needs the most substance, and Mauritius is built for that kind of activity.

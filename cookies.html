Cookie policy

Last updated: October 2026

This website is a simple information site. This page explains what it stores on your device, what third parties receive when you visit, and how you can control it.

Draft wording. [Counsel review required before publication.]

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small files that a website stores in your browser. Some are needed for a site to work, and others are used for analytics or advertising.

2. Cookies we set

This site does not set advertising, analytics or tracking cookies, and it does not use local storage to follow you between visits.

3. Third-party requests

The site loads its fonts from Google Fonts. When your browser requests the font files, Google can receive your IP address, the browser you use and the time of the request. Google handles that data under its own policies.

4. Emails you send us

If you email us, we receive your email address, the content of your message and the time it was sent. We use this information only to answer you and to manage your enquiry.

5. How to control cookies

  • Use your browser settings to block or delete cookies for this site.
  • Block third-party requests with a browser extension if you do not want Google Fonts to load.
  • Blocking Google Fonts only changes how the text looks. The site still works.

6. Changes to this policy

If we add cookies or tools that track visitors, we will update this policy and, where the law requires it, ask for your consent first.

7. Contact

Questions about this policy: sales@offshoreglobe.com.
